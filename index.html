// About

The player behind the projects

I wrote code before I managed it. I joined Applaunch building Flutter apps, and because I speak German, I ended up in the client calls, first translating, then running them. The delivery role started informally and became my job.

That background is the whole advantage. I can read a pull request, pressure-test an estimate, and tell when "two weeks" is really two days. Nothing gets lost in the gap between what engineering says and what the client hears, because I have stood on both sides of it.

Before any of this I ran an Instagram jersey business alone for four years: sourcing, marketing, sales, delivery. That is where I learned to hold ten things at once without dropping any. Same skill, more people involved now.

CASE FILE // MYSPORTCLUBS

A World Cup deadline that could not move.

December: kickoff on a six-month estimate. February: velocity inconsistent, carryover stacking sprint over sprint, team restructured. I took over delivery, rebaselined against the critical path, cut sprint scope until velocity held, and rebuilt a burndown people could trust. Shipped end of May with a team of 12 across design, frontend, backend and QA. The client's tournament campaign launched on schedule, and they have come back for six more platform versions since.
